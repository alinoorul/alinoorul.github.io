The World Is Yours


Why the name ZEROZENO?


Startups start at 0. The world knows them to be 0. From the outside, for a long time, 0 is all there is. The founder who keeps going, in the face of infinite effort, infinite halves, attains 1. This jump from 0 to 1 is unpredictable. The founder completing tasks does not know 1 will come, but dreams on. The founder who dreams and runs, wins irrationally against all odds.

A startup is expected to fail. A startup is expected to stay at 0. ZEROZENO captures a startup defying all odds and reaching 1 on the working principle of a founder never giving up.

Somewhere along the way when the founder is running their startup, the startup charts an unpredictable path to 1. Somewhere along the way, 1 becomes inevitable. ZEROZENO exists to finance a founder's journey till that unpredictable jump, when a startup starts up. This jump is what a startup lives for.

ZEROZENO is also named for Zeno's Dichotomy paradox. The reader is left to wonder why.

Exponential growth also differentiates startup from business. Somewhere in the growth of a startup, exponential growth must occur. Maybe in user growth, when existing users overwhelmingly recommend or attract more users. Maybe in revenue growth, when small initial sales snowball into bigger and bigger sales, like addictive apps. Maybe in network effects, where your product is the backbone of new applications. A startup must taste that sweet addictive nectar of exponential growth to be more than just a business.
